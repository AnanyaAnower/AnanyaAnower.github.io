Automated Makoto-Style Preprocessing Pipeline for High-Density EEG

Click any image to view it in full resolution and zoom.

Research Summary

Engineered a 16-step Makoto-style preprocessing pipeline in EEGLAB to analyze P300 ERP stability using 256-channel EGI datasets. While the full research project is currently an active pilot, I successfully implemented and validated the MATLAB scripts for automated ICA-based artifact rejection and signal cleaning.
